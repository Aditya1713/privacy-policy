Privacy Policy for Undercover

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Undercover ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent and safe gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

Undercover is designed with data minimization in mind. We do not require you to create an account with personal details like your real name, email address, or phone number.

  • Anonymous Identifier: The App uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a temporary unique identifier (UID) to your device. This is used solely to maintain game state, track turns, and connect you with other players in your room.
  • In-Game User Content: During gameplay, players enter a nickname, clues, votes, and in-game chat messages. This information is transmitted in real time to other players in the same room.
  • Connection Data: The App monitors your live connection status to detect when a player leaves or disconnects so the room state updates cleanly.

2. How We Use Information

The data collected is used solely to facilitate the core multiplayer gameplay:

  • To allow players to create, join, and interact in game rooms using 6-character room codes.
  • To distribute secret words, assign roles (Civilian, Undercover/Impostor), and calculate voting results.
  • To synchronize real-time clues and discussion among room participants.

3. Data Retention and Ephemeral Nature

Game room data is temporary (ephemeral).

All room records, active connections, votes, clues, and chat messages exist only for the duration of the active game session. When players leave or the game session ends, the room data is automatically removed from the database.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party infrastructure provided by Google to run the game:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database & Authentication: To synchronize real-time game state across devices securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). You can review Google's privacy policy at Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or gameplay data. Data is never shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that any personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
