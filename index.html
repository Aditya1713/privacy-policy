Privacy Policy for Undercover

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Undercover ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, fair, and safe social gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

Undercover is designed with strict data minimization in mind. We do not require you to create an account with personal details like your real name, email address, or phone number.

  • Anonymous Identifier: The App uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a temporary unique identifier (UID) to your device. This is used solely to maintain game state, track turns, and connect you with other players in your private room.
  • In-Game User Content: During gameplay, players submit temporary nicknames, single-word clues, votes, and in-game discussion messages. This content is transmitted in real time only to participants in that specific room.
  • Live Connection State: The App tracks active connection heartbeats to automatically handle player disconnects, maintain room integrity, and adjust player rosters.

2. User-Generated Content & Player Safety

Zero Tolerance Policy: Undercover strictly prohibits harassment, hate speech, vulgar profanity, sexually explicit content, and abusive behavior in player nicknames, clues, and room discussions.

To provide a welcoming environment and comply with platform User-Generated Content (UGC) safety requirements, the App provides built-in safety mechanisms:

  • Automated Content Filtering: Live chat and clue submissions are automatically filtered in real time for vulgar language, profanity, and offensive abbreviations. Messages attempting to reveal or leak the round's secret word are also automatically blocked.
  • In-App Muting (User Blocking): Players can tap any message to instantly mute an offending participant, which immediately hides all messages sent by that person for the remainder of the session.
  • In-App Reporting: Players can flag and report inappropriate messages or abusive behavior directly inside the active room.
  • Consensus Auto-Kick: To empower player rooms against griefing or disruptive behavior, when half or more of the room participants report the same player, that player is automatically removed from the room by group consensus.

3. How We Use Information

The data processed is used solely to facilitate the core multiplayer gameplay:

  • To allow players to create, join, and interact in game rooms using 6-character room codes.
  • To distribute secret words, assign roles (Civilian, Undercover/Impostor), and calculate voting results.
  • To synchronize real-time clues, discussion, and safety moderation events among room participants.

4. Data Retention and Ephemeral Nature

All game room data is temporary and ephemeral.

Room records, player connections, votes, clues, in-game chat messages, and reports exist only for the duration of the active game session. When the game ends or participants leave, room data is discarded and purged automatically from the active database.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted cloud infrastructure provided by Google to power the multiplayer experience:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database & Authentication: To synchronize real-time game state across devices securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). You can review Google's privacy policy at Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or gameplay data. Data is never shared with third-party advertisers, trackers, or data brokers.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that any personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game improvements or safety updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
